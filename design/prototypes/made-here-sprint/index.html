Three
directions.

Unpublished concepts for the owner to choose between. Each one is built in the site’s real styles, works in Night and Afterlight, and has working toys.

Read design/made-here-sprint.md for the critique, comparison and questions.

    The ledger

    Things first. A plain “Made here” list on the home page: every project, what it is, its status, and which questions it asks. Investigations are light collection pages. The playground sits beside the example study.
