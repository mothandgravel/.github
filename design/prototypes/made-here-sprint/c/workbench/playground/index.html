DRAWER 02

Did it happen, and can I take it back?

OPEN ↓CLOSE ↑

A press disappears. A reply never comes. A whole staircase goes down in one click. The work may already be done, and the way back has to match the thing someone believed they did.

SKYSTACK BUILDER / ASKS: DID IT HAPPEN?

One gesture. One undo.

Place a stamp on a small island. Every cell is checked before anything is spent. A placement becomes one entry in history, and Undo either takes the whole thing back or says plainly that it could not.

DRAWER 03

What does a green light promise?

OPEN ↓CLOSE ↑

Running. Passing. Finished. Each small light answers a different question, and the colour travels more easily than the meaning. We keep asking what a signal lets someone stop worrying about.

CYBER GRID / ASKS: WHAT DOES GREEN PROMISE?

Can this board be finished?

Cyber Grid checks every daily board for a route before anyone plays it. Here is that check on a smaller board. Place obstacles, then ask. Shrink the search budget and watch “not found” stop meaning “no”.
