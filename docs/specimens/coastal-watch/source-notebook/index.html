Why the rain is placed here METADATA-DERIVED / NOT SURVEYED

A place in the picture.
With the disagreement left in.

The source says Mercator, supplies four corners, and declares metre scales. Most of the size difference is compatible with ground/projected scaling in Mercator near 60° N. The two axis ratios and the lower-right latitude still do not reconcile under one uniform scale. We anchor the grid to its lower-left and upper-right corners in the supplied Mercator projection; the scales and conflicting lower-right latitude are retained but unused.

The agency’s example uses the same bounds directly in longitude and latitude. You can inspect that alternative for a selected cell. Neither interpretation makes a recorded radar cell a rain gauge at a buoy.

The coverage edge is checked against the two documented 250 km radar ranges. This tests internal geometric consistency, using the same source mask. It is not independent geolocation validation, a calibration, or a probability of accuracy.
