CALCULATION / EXPLICIT COVERAGE

Power becomes an estimate.

For each valid interval, E = (P₀ + P₁) × Δt ÷ 2. Power is in kW; Δt is in hours. Reported powers are treated as point samples; sensor averaging windows are not specified by the publisher. Boundaries are linearly interpolated. Intervals over 90 seconds, or with a missing/negative component, stay uncovered. No extrapolation or zero filling.

Facility energy is the sum of the five component energies. Period PUE is facility energy ÷ IT energy over the same covered intervals. Missing time is reported, not a statistical confidence bound.

INTERPRETATION / BOUNDED

A ratio has a boundary.

Sample PUE and the component sum are not independent sources. The source facility attributes some shared infrastructure, including a constant filter-pump allowance. We retain the published channels and add no further allowance.

No calibration uncertainty is supplied. The geometry is authored. Equipment count, rack demand and airflow are not inferred. Real-time lighting supports navigation. Refine light accumulates indirect illumination at a fixed viewpoint on hardware WebGL2 devices; reduced detail is used by software renderers. The rendered lighting is an artistic scheme, independent of the recorded weather and source clock.
