    THE BOUNDS OF THE STUDY

    A model you can disagree with.

    Battery starts at 62% of temperature-adjusted capacity. The fixed capacity allowance is 1 − 0.006 × (20 − ambient), clamped to 0.65–1. This is an illustrative assumption, not a cell manufacturer’s curve.

    10% stays reserved. Output is limited to 12 kW; charging to 8 kW. Discharge efficiency is 92%, charge efficiency 94%. The 18 kW generator has a three-minute warm-up and a 6 kW/minute ramp. Fuel is assumed sufficient for four hours.

    No electrical load flow, surge currents, voltage, frequency, starter energy, battery ageing, thermal dynamics or protection coordination are modelled. “Inspection” is a simulated operator declaration. No safety clearance or equipment control is implied.

    WHAT WE BROUGHT FORWARD

    The circuit changes.
    The question stays.

    Coastal watch asks what each station knows after an interruption. Here the interruption consumes something. Waiting costs energy, and an old permission can outlive the conditions that made it reasonable.

    Raivari supplies the unmodified rate limiter, clamp, register addresses and value encoder in this browser. The energy balance, load choices and restart receipt model are new work for this specimen.

    The maritime battery behaviour is not presented as a land-site power solver. Its fixed capacity and measurement noise stay out of this energy account. The pinned source record shows exactly what travelled with us.

    An independent design study in field-support resilience. Synthetic conditions, explicit decisions, inspectable consequences.
