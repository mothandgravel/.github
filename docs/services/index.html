Interface design and software engineering.

Moth & Gravel designs interfaces and builds the software behind them. For product teams and founders, our work covers cloud and mobile products, connected devices, and the interfaces people use to supervise automated work.

Clearer workflows, status displays and controls for products people rely on. Start by annotating the screen that causes hesitation or prototyping a better sequence.
