Three small working pieces from our own projects. Each is a reduction: one rule, honestly cut down, so you can try the idea in a minute.

Nothing you do here is saved or sent.

CYBER GRID / A ROUTE CHECK, REDUCED

Can this board be finished?

Cyber Grid checks every daily board for a route before anyone plays it. Here is that check on a smaller board. Place obstacles, then ask. Shrink the search budget and watch “not found” stop meaning “no”.

What does a green light promise? ↗

This toy needs JavaScript. In words: the search either finds a route you can check, proves there is none, or runs out of steps. Running out is not a no.

A 5 × 5 reduction using the game’s jump rule and route search, restyled. The game itself plays on a larger board.

SKYSTACK BUILDER / ONE UNDO

One gesture. One undo.

Place a stamp on a small island. Every cell is checked before anything is spent. A placement becomes one entry in history, and Undo either takes the whole thing back or says plainly that it could not.

Did it happen, and can I take it back? ↗

This toy needs JavaScript. In words: a stamp is placed whole or not at all, and one undo takes back the whole stamp, or refuses and keeps its record.

A rules sketch written from the source study in Field Note 008. No game code, art or assets; not a capture of SkyStack Builder.

DECISION DOCKET / TWO SHAPES

The same week, in two shapes.

Six fictional items in the manner of a Decision Docket recap, drawn as a river and as a ring. Each one carries the revision it was captured at. Change the shape and watch what the drawing implies about time.

How old is what I’m seeing? ↗

This toy needs JavaScript. In words: six fictional items from one week, each with its captured revision; one still waits on you, outside the week.

Authored, fictional items. Not a capture of any real workspace, and not the product’s interface.
