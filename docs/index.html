04 — THE PERSON AT THE BENCHPERTTU SLIDEN / INDEPENDENT WORKS

Many projects.
One curiosity.

Behind Moth & Gravel is Perttu Sliden, a software architect with more than 25 years spent making complex systems hold together.

The work moves between screens and the things they touch: cloud services, mobile software, relays, microcontrollers and physical terminals. Some questions arrive as code. Others arrive with wires.

AI is part of the making; architecture, constraints and judgment stay close to hand. Across a pipeline that includes our Navivra projects, Field Notes keep the useful discoveries and stubborn questions in view. The questions travel between projects. So do we.
