04 — THE PERSON AT THE BENCHSLIDEEP / FINLAND

Many projects.
One curiosity.

Behind Moth & Gravel is slideep: a maker of strange, useful things. More than 25 years spent getting complicated systems to work together—and still drawn to the odd question.

The work moves between screens and the things they touch: cloud services, mobile software, relays, microcontrollers and physical terminals. Some questions arrive as code. Others arrive with wires.

AI is part of the making; architecture, constraints and judgment stay close to hand. Field Notes keep the useful discoveries and stubborn questions in view. The questions travel between projects. So do we.

What does the studio make?

Independent software and useful tools. The work crosses cloud services, mobile interfaces, microcontrollers and physical devices. Field Notes show the design questions inside that work.

Where is Moth & Gravel based?

Based in Finland. Open to questions from anywhere. The studio’s public record lives here: source, materials and studies from across the workbench.

How can I get in touch?

Bring us a question. Tell us what you are trying to make and where it gets difficult. Give your question a little shape and keep an editable brief, or write to us through a public GitHub issue. The GitHub route requires an account; keep private details out of it.
