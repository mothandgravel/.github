This packet adds evidence beside the original Coastal watch capture, the evening source recheck and the fire evidence. The earlier packets preserve their original state and their checksums, including the earlier finding that the video had yet to be verified.

- **Firefighting footage.** Yle item `64-3-313886` embeds the [source post](https://x.com/Merivartiosto/status/2124217067708522514/video/1). The Gulf of Finland Coast Guard published it at 19:03:09 UTC (21:03:09 EET) and credits Alfons Håkans for the footage. Yle published its item at 19:31:39.791983 UTC. The saved MP4 was decoded for visual inspection and shows water spray directed at the named ship at night. The reviewed frames contain no visible camera time. The container creation field and X muxer tag describe the distributed MP4. The original recording time and camera timezone remain unknown. This packet links to the public publisher and preserves the video hash and retrieval receipt. The clip remains on its source platform.
- **Daylight still.** The official JPEG has the visible camera string `21/11/26 16:38:09:08`. The exact same source bytes contain EXIF Original and Digitized times of 15:48:16 with explicit offset +02:00 (13:48:16 UTC). This is a different timestamp from the displayed clock. EET fits the ordering of the served JPEG’s Last-Modified value better than UTC. The [HTTP specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#name-last-modified) defines that header for the selected representation. The header gives no setting for the original camera. The timezone and final field of the displayed string remain unknown.

The JW player response reports 50 seconds and a `pubdate` of 12:13:58 UTC. The embedded `VideoObject.uploadDate` labels differ between article pages and are retained verbatim. Their meaning beyond publication metadata is unverified. The supplied object URL belongs to its originating browser context, and the exact blob could not be matched to the player item. Only the media provenance, labels and source hashes are included.
