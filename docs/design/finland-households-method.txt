TWO HOMES / A SEPARATE HOUSEHOLD COMPARISON
Method version 2026-10-23.2. Checked 23 October 2026.

Published source records and original input fingerprints remain in
finland-provenance.json. The pinned Finnish extract is unchanged.

These are editable scenarios, not measured homes or population averages.
Defaults: apartment electricity 3,000 kWh/year; electrically heated home
20,000 kWh/year. Apartment electricity excludes separately allocated district
heat. The electric home includes heating and has no district-heat connection.
Every source-band selection uses total annual electricity, not exposed use.

ELECTRICITY
Baseline = annual kWh x selected-month published total c/kWh / 100.
Baseline component prices already contain the published tax allocation. No VAT
or separate fixed charges are added to that unit-price account.

Exposed kWh = annual kWh x chosen exposure percentage / 100.
Gross change EUR = exposed kWh x chosen VAT-inclusive price change c/kWh / 100.
Net energy change = gross change / (1 + source-month VAT rate).
VAT change = gross change - net energy change.
After component = baseline component + component change.
Network and electricity excise do not change in this branch. The baseline's
published component-rounding residual is preserved. No demand response occurs.

VAT: 24% through August 2024; 25.5% from September 2024 within the pinned
2024-2026 price window. Source: Finnish Tax Administration, rate history:
https://www.vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/verot-ja-maksut/arvonlisaverotus/arvonlisaveroprosentit/
The shock is VAT-inclusive. Splitting it does not add another tax charge.

The exposed share is a share of kWh, not calendar months. Zero means an
unchanged retail energy rate is assumed for all use. It is not a claim that
every fixed contract protects against tax or network changes. A partial share
does not invent an hourly or seasonal profile. The shock is not a predicted
effect of data centres. Historical month changes are not causal comparisons.

HEAT
Default: a connected apartment building is assumed, with its tariff consequence
unreported. The direct benefit and combined change remain null, not zero.
A verified pipe alone establishes neither a recovered-heat quantity nor savings.

Optional lower-charge branch:
  allocated thermal MWh/year x chosen VAT-inclusive reduction EUR/MWh
  x chosen fraction reaching the household.
Defaults inside this optional branch: 7.5 MWh, 10 EUR/MWh, 100% pass-through.
All are authored, including the thermal allocation. Fixed heat charges are
unchanged; there is no complete heat bill or seasonal heat model.

An unverified connection leaves the benefit null. No connection gives zero
direct reduction through that district-heat network in this scenario. It says
nothing about indirect electricity-market or wider economic effects.
An assumed unchanged tariff gives zero tariff reduction, labelled as assumed.
Even an assumed lower charge does not establish a saving caused by server heat:
attributedToRecoveredHeatEUR and measuredHouseholdSavingEUR always remain null.

Nivos's 2024 residential use is 16.0 GWh, Taul5!R138. Its source-to-home recovered
heat attribution is unavailable. Nivos's 2025 annual report, PDF page 6, reports
15% lower summer energy charges from 1 May 2025 and 5% lower winter energy
charges from 1 January 2026. Those reported tariff-component changes are not
inserted into the household scenario or treated as full-bill/causal savings.

COMBINED CHANGE
Electricity change minus separately quantified household heat-charge reduction.
This is a change across separate charges, not a complete household bill. Where
the heat consequence is unknown the apartment's combined change remains null.
Heat is never deducted from the electricity account or national grid demand.

EXPORT
finland-inspection@2 adds householdComparison, preserving the existing accounts,
original source locations, source fingerprints and reuse notices. It contains
both homes, price exposure, source-month VAT, all heat assumptions, null unknowns,
formulae and the separate recorded local heat context. Invalid inputs suppress
results and disable export rather than exporting the previous valid branch.
