PRICE / monthly national household consumer bands, January 2024–June 2026.
Energy and network components exclude tax. The published tax component includes
electricity excise and VAT; no VAT is added again. Annual use is authored.
The selected monthly price held for a year is an annualised illustration, not
a household bill, a contract offer, a forecast, or a data-centre causal effect.
Each exported price record includes the zero-based JSON-stat value indices.

GRID / two complete recorded weeks, 1–7 January and 1–7 July 2024.
336 one-hour mean-power intervals. Publisher fixed normal time is UTC+2, even
in July. Source labels 1–24 are interpreted as hour-ending slots. That explicit
normalisation is retained alongside the original date, hour, sheet and row.
The annual source validation covers all 8,784 intervals of leap year 2024.
Energy uses sum(MW × 1 hour); no trapezoid, daylight-saving adjustment or gap fill.
Exports remain negative. Wind is included in production, never added again.
The additional-load branch is arithmetic at fixed historical supply. It does not
model dispatch, imports, spare grid capacity, a blackout or a price response.

HEAT / Nivos company/municipal row 114, 2024 district-heat statistics.
Thermal GWh remain distinct from electrical MWh. Recovery / heat-pump output
is one combined category. It is not pure server heat. Residential customer use
is reported directly in Taul5!R138, not inferred from the source mix. Heat to
industry and other customers is kept separate. Losses include meter differences.
Heat-production electricity is '..' in the source and remains unavailable.
The first supply category keeps its original Finnish label in the receipt;
the interface calls it other net heat production, without treating it all as
combustion. A source-to-residential attribution of recovered heat is unavailable.

2025 company-wide heat sales are a separate report, with no residential split
in this extract. The 41% recovered-heat origin disclosure concerns only central
Mäntsälä; it is never multiplied by company-wide sales or used as a tariff saving.

Data-source checks do not validate physical meters or the causal impact of a
particular data-centre investment. The existing ESIF facility study is a separate
US account and its authored room makes no claim to depict a Finnish facility.
