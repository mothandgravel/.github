The Ageing Reading

Streaming study

How old is the reading?

A connection can remain joined while the last measurement grows old. Put “connected” beside that number and the eye can quietly lend one fact to the other.

WHAT WE KEEP SEPARATEThe connection. The measurement. Its age.

At eighteen seconds, the study’s connection is still marked connected. Its measurement is stale. A recent sample would still leave picture usefulness open.

The decision we are trying: keep the old number available, with its age beside it. That adds a little reading. Whether it helps someone choose their next move needs a human trial.

An old reading can look current. A missing reply leaves a different uncertainty: the work may already be done.

The Missing Reply

Ajo
Recorded simulator case

No answer.
Do we try again?

Ajo explores how agent plans are checked and their execution recorded. In this saved case, the receiver has applied the operation once. The host is still missing its answer.

WHAT WE KEEP SEPARATEThe action. Its reply.

A missing reply does not establish that nothing happened. In this case, querying the original result recovers the answer while receiver applications stay at one.

The interface question: what should the person supervising the work see before choosing a next move? A general “try again” leaves too much unsaid.

Both pieces ask the surface to admit what it knows. A small green light has the same obligation.

The Reassuring Signal

Field note 005
Across the workbench

One small light.
A large promise.

Running. Passing. Delivered. Three encouraging words, each answering a different question. The colour travels easily between them. The meaning needs more care.

What can this indicator honestly let someone stop worrying about?

Our field note follows that question through Geaidnu, Ajo and Paja. A healthy runtime needs its own boundary. A passing test can correctly preserve an unknown outcome. A build result can make a precise claim about the steps it ran.

WHAT WE KEEP SEPARATEThe observation. The conclusion.

Name the question the signal answers. Keep the route to its evidence nearby. Give what remains unknown a place to stay.

A thread through source-based studies and a saved simulator record. It establishes neither a shared shipped interface nor how people understand these distinctions in use.
