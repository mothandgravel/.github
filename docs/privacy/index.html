In plain words.

What happens to a message you send us, who handles it on the way, and what you can ask us to do about it.

What we collect, and why

This site has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We hold personal data only when you write to us.

  • The enquiry form (the last step of Leave a loose end): your name, your email address and the brief you wrote, including anything personal you put in it. The page also gets a Cloudflare check that you are not a bot, which sees your IP address and browser details.
  • Email you send us directly, or through the “Or email us instead” link: whatever your own mail app puts in the message, including your address.

We use this only to read your message, reply to you, and, if you want to work together, take the steps that come before an agreement. We do not use it for marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not add you to a list.

Legal basis. Replying to your enquiry and preparing for possible work is a step you ask for before a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering people who write to us (Article 6(1)(f)). The bot check rests on our legitimate interest in keeping the form from being flooded with automated messages (Article 6(1)(f)). Giving us your details is your choice, but without a name and an email address we cannot reply.

The brief you write stays in your browser tab until you press Send. Nothing is sent as you type.

Services that handle it, and where

These services process your data for us. We do not share it with anyone else.

Cloudflare Turnstile (bot check)
Loaded only when you start filling in the form. Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser signals to decide whether you are a person, and gives the page a one-time token. Our server checks that token with Cloudflare and does not pass your IP address on. Cloudflare processes data worldwide. [TO CONFIRM: transfer safeguards and what Cloudflare stores in your browser]
Google Cloud Run, Finland region (our relay)
A small service receives the form, checks it, and passes it on by email. It keeps no copy of your message and writes no names, addresses or message text to its logs. Google’s platform records routine request details such as time and IP address. [TO CONFIRM: platform log retention]
Resend (sending the email)
Resend sends your message to us from mail.mothandgravel.com, with your address as the reply-to. It handles the sender, recipient, subject and text, and keeps delivery records. [TO CONFIRM: Resend processing location, retention setting and transfer safeguards]
OVH (our mailbox)
OVH receives mail sent to hello@mothandgravel.com and forwards it to the studio inbox. It handles every message to that address, including ones sent from your own mail app. [TO CONFIRM: OVH service location, and the mailbox provider it forwards to]
GitHub Pages (hosting this site)
GitHub serves these pages and, like any web host, sees the address and browser details of each visit in its own server logs. We do not receive those logs. GitHub processes data in the United States and elsewhere. [TO CONFIRM: GitHub transfer safeguards]

If you use the email link instead, your own mail provider carries the message to us.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry does not become work, we delete the message and your details 12 months after the last message between us [TO CONFIRM: retention period]. If it becomes work, we keep what the agreement and the law require, such as accounting records, for as long as they require. The services above keep their own short-lived technical records, as described there.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you (access);
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it (erasure);
  • stop using it on the basis of our legitimate interest (objection).

Write to hello@mothandgravel.com. We will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutettu) at tietosuoja.fi ↗, or to the authority in your own country.

Cookies and local storage

  • Cookies: this site sets none, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. All fonts, images, audio and video come from this site itself.
  • Theme choice: if you press the theme switch, your choice (Night, Afterlight or System) is saved in your browser’s local storage so the next page matches. It never leaves your device.
  • This visit only: your draft brief and any example you pin are kept in your browser tab’s session storage, and a flag remembers that you have seen the moth animation. They disappear when the tab closes.
  • Cloudflare: the bot check loads from Cloudflare only after you start the form. [TO CONFIRM: whether it stores anything in your browser]
  • Links to GitHub and other sites leave this site; their notices apply there.

Changes

If we change what we do with your data, we update this page and its date. Last updated: 18 October 2026.
