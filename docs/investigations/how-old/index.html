01 / RAIVARI

Raivari
Prototype

Two clocks,
one picture.

Raivari is the framework under our Coastal watch and maritime studies. In Coastal watch, a message has the time it was observed and the time it was received. A recently received message can describe an older position, so the two ages stay separate.

When the stream stops, the last positions stay on the chart and their age keeps growing beside them. In the maritime study, observed weather, a depicted night and a ship held still each keep their own label.

02 / LIVECONNECT

LiveConnect
Study, parked

Connected
is not current.

A streaming study, now parked. Its code keeps the state of the connection apart from the age of a measurement. The link can hold while the reading grows old.

The specimen lets you turn an inspection clock and watch one reassuring label say too much, beside a version that gives the connection, the measurement and its age a place each.

03 / DECISION DOCKET

Decision Docket
In production for our own work

Captured,
at a revision.

Our own tool for the decisions and evidence behind software work, in production for our own work. A claim about a source carries its address and the revision it was captured at.

A captured revision is a fixed point. The recap can say what was true then; it should not suggest that nothing has changed since.

Shown here only through fictional examples. The real workspace holds private work.

04 / MAPPING BUGGY

Mapping buggy
Study

The last packet,
and now.

A small robot in a cardboard arena, and the maps and gauges it leaves behind. Its telemetry dashboard keeps the latest reading on screen; after half a second without a packet, the label changes from Connected to Stale data while the gauges keep their last values.

Its map starts every cell undecided. Free, occupied and not yet known stay three different answers, so a quiet patch of floor does not pass for evidence.
